# Target Market Segments - Parcel AI

## Market Segmentation Overview

Our target market consists of **enterprise organizations with dedicated land acquisition teams** that make significant land investment decisions. We focus on segments where comprehensive intelligence provides clear ROI and competitive advantage.

## Primary Target Segments

### 1. Real Estate Developers (Enterprise)

**Profile:**
- Organizations: Large regional and national real estate development firms
- Annual Land Acquisition Budget: $50M - $1B+
- Team Size: 5-50 land acquisition professionals
- Project Types: Residential, commercial, mixed-use developments

**Key Characteristics:**
- Multiple concurrent acquisition projects
- Competitive markets with tight timelines
- Need for standardized processes across teams
- Focus on deal flow and velocity

**Pain Points:**
- Information scattered across multiple sources
- Time pressure to evaluate opportunities quickly
- Need to track competitive activity
- Portfolio-level decision making

**Value Proposition:**
- 10x faster initial parcel research
- Identify opportunities before competitors
- Standardize evaluation process across teams
- Portfolio-level analytics and insights

**Decision Makers:**
- VP/Director of Land Acquisition
- VP/Director of Real Estate Development
- Chief Investment Officer

### 3. Retail & Restaurant Chains (Site Selection)

**Profile:**

- Expansion Rate: 10-100+ new locations annually

**Key Characteristics:**

- Specific location criteria (traffic, demographics, competition)

**Pain Points:**

- Time-consuming market analysis
- Difficulty evaluating multiple markets
- Need for demographic and traffic data integration

**Value Proposition:**
- Rapid site identification and screening
- Integrated demographic and market data
- Multi-market comparison tools
- Standardized evaluation framework

**Decision Makers:**
- VP/Director of Real Estate

- Head of Site Selection

## Market Sizing

### Total Addressable Market (TAM)
- Enterprise organizations in North America with land acquisition needs
- Estimated: 10,000+ organizations
- Total market value: $500M - $1B annually

### Serviceable Addressable Market (SAM)
- Organizations in primary segments with teams of 5+ professionals
- Estimated: 2,000 - 3,000 organizations
- Market value: $200M - $400M annually

### Serviceable Obtainable Market (SOM) - 3 Years
- Realistic market capture in primary segments
- Target: 100-200 enterprise customers
- Market value: $10M - $30M annually

## Ideal Customer Profile (ICP)

### Firmographic Criteria
- **Industry:** Real estate development, investment, retail, infrastructure
- **Company Size:** $100M+ annual revenue
- **Land Acquisition Budget:** $20M+ annually
- **Team Size:** 5+ dedicated land acquisition professionals
- **Geographic Focus:** Major metropolitan markets in North America

### Behavioral Criteria
- Active land acquisition activity (multiple deals per year)
- Data-driven decision-making culture
- Willingness to adopt new technology
- Budget for professional tools and software
- Pain with current research process

### Technical Criteria
- Modern technology stack
- Cloud software adoption
- API integration capability (for larger customers)
- Security and compliance requirements

---

## Anti-Personas (Who We Don't Target)

### Individual Investors / Small Developers
- Insufficient budget for enterprise pricing
- Limited deal volume doesn't justify platform
- Better served by consumer-grade tools

### Government Agencies (Initially)
- Long, complex sales cycles
- Specific procurement requirements
- Better suited for Year 2-3 expansion

### International Markets (Initially)
- Data coverage limitations
- Regulatory differences
- Focus on North America first
