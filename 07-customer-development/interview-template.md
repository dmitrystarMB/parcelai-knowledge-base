## Interview Structure

### Introduction (5 minutes)
- Thank them for their time
- Explain purpose of conversation
- Set expectations (duration, recording)
- Get permission to take notes/record

### Background (10 minutes)
**Goal:** Understand their role and context

Questions:
- Tell me about your role and responsibilities
- How does land acquisition fit into your organization?
- Walk me through your typical land acquisition process
- Who else is involved in these decisions?

---

### Problem Discovery (20 minutes)
**Goal:** Understand pain points and challenges

Questions:
- What are the biggest challenges in your land acquisition process?
- How much time do you typically spend on research and analysis?
- What information do you need to make acquisition decisions?
- Where do you get that information today?
- What frustrates you most about current tools/processes?
- What would make your job significantly easier?

---

### Solution Validation (15 minutes)
**Goal:** Test assumptions about our solution

Questions:
- If you could wave a magic wand, what would the perfect solution look like?
- [Show mockups/demo if appropriate]
- How would this fit into your workflow?
- What features are must-haves vs. nice-to-haves?
- What concerns do you have about [our approach]?

---

### Economic Validation (5 minutes)
**Goal:** Understand budget and decision process

Questions:
- What do you currently spend on data and tools for this?
- Who controls the budget for new tools?
- What's your approval process for new software?
- What ROI would you need to see to justify investment?

---

### Closing (5 minutes)
- Thank them for their time
- Ask if okay to follow up with questions
- Gauge interest in staying involved
- Ask for referrals to others
