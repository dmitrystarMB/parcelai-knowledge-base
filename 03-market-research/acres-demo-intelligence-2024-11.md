# Acres.com Demo Intelligence

**Date:** November 2024
**Threat Level:** 🔴 Critical

---

## Executive Summary

Acres.com demonstrated a mature, comprehensive land intelligence platform with $15-30M ARR and 500K users. Strong product with critical features Parcel AI lacks: LLC unmasking, power infrastructure data (94K+ substations), data center sales comps, and hyperscaler tracking. PNK showed very high buying intent, particularly for power/fiber infrastructure and competitive intelligence. Key Acres weaknesses: 30-day update lag, no industrial tracking, Pennsylvania non-disclosure data gaps, and acknowledged zoning challenges - all opportunities for differentiation.

---

## Top Features Demonstrated

1. **LLC Unmasking**: AI-powered entity resolution reveals beneficial owners behind shell LLCs; tracks "few dozen" hyperscalers; maps 219 parcels to Amazon Data Services Inc.; detects acquisitions 18-36 months before public announcements

2. **Power Infrastructure Intelligence**: 94,000+ substations with voltage filtering (230V+, 345V+); transmission lines with capacity/ownership; retired power plants tracking; reduces 94K → 10K viable sites instantly

3. **Data Center Intelligence Index**: Tracks hyperscaler land banking (Meta, Amazon, Google, Microsoft); "Stealth" vs. "Verified" buys; 4 status types (Active, Under Construction, Planned, Land Banked); Meta Iowa Q3 2024 example shown

4. **Fiber Connectivity**: Current + future fiber lines; market-dependent coverage; Ohio market shown with data center correlation; "Data centers are just peppered along the fiber lines"

5. **Sales Comparables**: Data center-specific comps; weekly curation + monthly courthouse sales; proprietary non-disclosure state data (12 states); essential for underwriting

6. **Multi-Layer Filtering**: Infrastructure-first workflow (power → voltage → geography → acreage); demonstrated 94K substations → 110 viable parcels in Georgia; "disqualify land faster"

7. **Asset Intelligence ("Spider Web")**: Click entity → view all properties nationwide; Amazon example: 219 parcels, 44 purchases; reverse lookup by owner name

8. **Retired Power Plants**: Filter 3,500 plants by retirement year; "past 5 years" → 684 opportunities; trend: "Big players using infrastructure from retired power plants"

9. **Environmental Due Diligence**: Elevation, flood/wetland, soil types, building permits, property tax, land cover; comprehensive parcel-level data

10. **Natural Gas Infrastructure**: Pipeline overlays; important for power generation/backup; combinable with other layers

6. **Infrastructure-Based Search**: Multi-layer filtering starting with power/fiber/utilities; demonstrated workflow (94K → 110 parcels); we have basic filtering, not infrastructure-first

## Our Advantages ⭐

1. **Real-Time Data**: Acres updates monthly (30-day cadence); we can deliver event-driven updates (new permit → instant notification); major speed advantage

2. **Multi-Vertical Tracking**: Acres limited to data centers + homebuilders; industrial transactions NOT tracked (PNK asked, Acres said custom dataset needed); we can build broad from day one

3. **AI-Powered Zoning Monitoring**: Acres acknowledged "updates constantly, changing everything every month" - difficult to maintain; automated change detection opportunity

4. **Workflow Automation**: Acres has manual consultative process (~2 week turnaround); we have project management (Module 1); better pipeline workflow potential

5. **Non-Disclosure State ML**: Pennsylvania (PNK's primary market) has only 2 transactions in Acres; ML to infer pricing from alternative data sources

6. **Self-Serve + Enterprise Hybrid**: Acres is enterprise-only; lower barrier to entry with mid-market tier ($99-$999/month); product-led growth engine

## Key Learnings

### About Market:
1. **Power infrastructure is #1 constraint** for data center development; voltage filtering (230V+, 345V+) essential; 94K substations → 10K viable sites
2. **Fiber connectivity critical** for AI workloads; low-latency requirements; correlation with data center locations obvious
3. **18-36 month lag** between land acquisition and public announcement; early detection = massive competitive advantage
4. **Non-disclosure states** (13 in US) have severe data limitations; Pennsylvania (PNK market) only has 2 Acres transactions
5. **Retired power plants** emerging trend; existing grid connections + land; "Big players using infrastructure from retired plants"

### About Customer Needs (PNK):
1. **"Information is like gold"** - data scarcity is major pain point
2. **Pre-screening scale** needed: "hundreds of parcels" in pipeline; must reduce engineering costs
3. **Zoning complexity** is major current pain: "Go through every county, township, ordinance map" manually
4. **Speed-to-market** valued: they own contractors, need tools to move fast
5. **Multi-vertical portfolio**: data centers + industrial; Acres can't serve industrial needs

### About Technical Approach:
1. **Infrastructure-first search** resonates: start with power/fiber constraints, then find parcels
2. **Entity resolution requires** years of data + AI/ML; 500K user patterns improve algorithms; manual verification layer
3. **Network effects** matter: more users → better entity unmasking; hard to replicate
4. **Curated data beats automation**: weekly manual curation + monthly courthouse sales; human judgment essential
5. **Zoning is hard problem**: constant changes defeat automation; opportunity for AI-powered monitoring
