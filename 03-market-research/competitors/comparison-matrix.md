## Competitive Battle Cards

### Quick Reference

#### When Customer Says: "We use [Data Provider]"
**Response:**
"That's great for basic property data. Parcel AI builds on that foundation by providing intelligence and analytics purpose-built for acquisition decisions. Our customers using [Data Provider] found they were spending weeks manually analyzing data - we reduce that to hours while providing better insights."

**Follow-up:**
- Demo integrated analytics
- Show time savings
- Discuss ROI

---

#### When Customer Says: "We built our own tools"
**Response:**
"That shows you understand the value of good tools. Many of our customers started that way and found the ongoing maintenance and evolution became a distraction from their core business. Let's discuss the total cost of ownership and opportunity cost."

**Follow-up:**
- TCO analysis
- Feature velocity comparison
- Market expansion discussion
- Technical debt conversation

---

#### When Customer Says: "Too expensive"
**Response:**
"I understand. Let's quantify the value. If your team spends X hours per week on research at $Y per hour, and we save 50-70% of that time, what's that worth? Plus the value of faster decisions and competitive advantage."

**Follow-up:**
- ROI calculator
- Time savings examples
- Competitive advantage value
- Risk reduction value
