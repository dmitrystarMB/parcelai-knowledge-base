# [[Acres.com]] - vertebrae

**Problem + How we solve it:**
Land professionals waste weeks manually researching fragmented data across 3,000+ county systems, miss critical opportunities because they can’t see through shell entities, and discover too late that Amazon secretly bought land for a data center 18 months ago or that competitors are already land-banking in tomorrow’s growth corridors. Acres.com solves this by aggregating 150+ million parcels into one searchable platform with AI-powered entity unmasking that reveals who owns what and what they paid—tracking hyperscaler acquisitions, institutional moves, and residential development patterns 18-36 months before public announcements, so you know where the smart money is moving before everyone else does.

**What are you building and for whom?**

Acres.com is a land intelligence platform that brings transparency to America's $26 trillion land market. We provide comprehensive data on 150+ million parcels—including ownership, comparable sales, and predictive analytics—for land brokers, homebuilders, developers, and institutional investors. When a homebuilder needs to identify the next growth corridor before land prices spike, or when a data center developer needs to find 500-acre sites with gigawatt power access, they use Acres to cut research time from weeks to minutes.

**The origin story: From internal tool to category leader**

Carter Malloy grew up on farmland in Arkansas and later worked as an equity research analyst at Stephens Inc. In 2018, he founded AcreTrader—a fractional farmland investment platform that raised $82.5M and deployed $408M+ across 140+ properties. To underwrite deals, his team needed comprehensive land intelligence, but no such platform existed. They built internal tools to aggregate parcel data, unmask shell entities, and track comparable sales across all 50 states—including non-disclosure states. In October 2022, they launched this internal tool publicly as Acres.com. Within three years, Acres became larger than the original AcreTrader business. In August 2025, Proterra Investment Partners acquired AcreTrader for its farmland portfolio, while Carter retained Acres.com to focus on the high-growth data business.

**Why hasn't this been done before?**

Land data is inherently fragmented across 3,000+ county courthouses with no standardization. Non-disclosure states hide transaction prices. Shell entities obscure ownership—40% of land is held through LLCs where the true buyer is invisible. CoStar dominates commercial buildings but ignores raw land. County GIS systems have terrible UX and can't search across jurisdictions. It took us five years to build relationships with county recorders, standardize messy data, develop entity-unmasking AI, and curate proprietary transaction records in states where prices aren't public. Cloud infrastructure and modern AI finally make this possible at scale—but only if you've invested years building the data moat we have.

**Why is this hard to do what you are doing?**

We've built multi-year competitive moats that are nearly impossible to replicate. First, data network effects: 500,000 users generate search patterns that improve our entity-unmasking algorithms daily. Second, curation infrastructure: we maintain relationships with 3,000+ counties and hand-verify ownership chains that automated scrapers miss. Third, proprietary  we've collected transaction prices in 12 non-disclosure states through manual research and partnerships that took years to establish. Fourth, regulatory complexity: navigating data licensing agreements across 50 states with different privacy laws creates barriers. Our founder built the original platform to solve his own problem investing in farmland—this isn't a venture studio idea, it's battle-tested by someone who underwritten hundreds of millions in land deals.

**Why is this an opportunity not to be missed?**

We launched publicly in October 2022. We already have 500,000 accounts, we're growing triple-digits year-over-year, and we've hit $15-30M+ in annual recurring revenue. Top 10 national homebuilders like Century Communities are replacing 5-10 legacy tools with Acres. We're closing enterprise deals at $50K-150K+ annually while freemium growth continues accelerating. The $26 trillion U.S. land market transacts $150-300B annually, yet there's no Bloomberg Terminal equivalent. Six tailwinds are converging: AI infrastructure driving unprecedented land demand, a 4-7 million unit housing shortage, generational farmland wealth transfer ($2.3T over 20 years), climate-driven site selection complexity, and PropTech adoption acceleration post-COVID. By the time Amazon announces a data center, they bought the land 18-36 months earlier through shell entities—our Data Center Index tracks these acquisitions in real-time. When builders discover our Home Builder Index predicts residential growth corridors 3-5 years early, they can't go back to manual research. We're not just building software—we're creating the essential infrastructure layer for every land decision in America. In January 2025, Carter announced: "Acres has become an even larger business than the original AcreTrader business." The separation from Proterra's acquisition allows us to focus entirely on becoming the category-defining land intelligence platform, and we're just getting started.

# Acres is organized around:

- **Parcel & ownership:** owner names, addresses, assessor data, legal descriptions. [acres.com](https://www.acres.com/pricing)
    
- **Sales & valuation:** courthouse sales, a staff‑**curated** sales database (updated **weekly**), and tools to build valuation reports; courthouse sales are updated **monthly**. [support.acres.com](https://support.acres.com/migration/filter-comparable-sales-data?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Layers (risk & infrastructure):** flood, elevation/terrain, soils, zoning, utilities, and power grid layers such as **transmission lines** and **substations** (with details like voltage, capacity, status, and ownership). [landvalues.acres.com+1](https://landvalues.acres.com/map-highlight-us-transmission-lines-map?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Acres AI (“entity unmasking”):** connects **parcels, transactions, and mortgages** to the entities behind them to reveal who actually controls the land. [landvalues.acres.com](https://landvalues.acres.com/ai-powered-land-sourcing-diligence)
    
- **Team/enterprise tools:** API/CRM integrations, data export, mortgage data, “Assets Intel” (links properties, owner names, debt, and purchases), and portfolio collaboration. [acres.com](https://www.acres.com/pricing)
    
- **Mobile + offline:** iOS/Android app with offline areas, route tracking, 3D maps, photo geotagging, and waypoints. [App Store+2acres.com+2](https://apps.apple.com/us/app/acres-land-data-parcel-maps/id1629489295?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

> **Data freshness:** update cadence varies by data type and county. Acres’ FAQ notes **parcel records & historical sales update monthly**; curated comps weekly; some infrastructures are annual (e.g., USDA crop history). Practically, “real‑time” for recorded transactions is bounded by county publication schedules.

# How the **Data Center Index** actually works

Think of the Index as two engines working together: (1) **acquisition detection** for hyperscaler/colocation moves; and (2) **site‑suitability context** to show why those purchases matter.

### 1) Acquisition detection (the “who bought what” layer)

**Inputs & frequency**

- **Courthouse sales** (from county assessor/recorder sources): ingested and normalized; update cadence generally **monthly**. [support.acres.com](https://support.acres.com/migration/filter-comparable-sales-data?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Curated sales**: Acres’ in‑house team adds and verifies additional transactions **weekly** (helps especially in non‑disclosure states). [support.acres.com](https://support.acres.com/migration/filter-comparable-sales-data?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Mortgages & deeds:** surfaced to users and linked to parcels (Enterprise); selections can include **Mortgages** and **Deed groups** in maps. [support.acres.com](https://support.acres.com/making-a-selection?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

**Entity unmasking**

- **Acres AI** connects **parcels, transactions, and mortgages to the entities behind them** to reveal beneficial ownership and cross‑jurisdiction footprints (e.g., shells/LLCs used by hyperscalers). The platform explicitly positions “**entity unmasking**” as a core capability for the Index. [landvalues.acres.com+1](https://landvalues.acres.com/ai-powered-land-sourcing-diligence)
    

**What the Index delivers**

- **Verified land sales tied to data center activity** and **entity unmasking** to “reveal real buyers behind entities,” with **monthly reports on hyperscaler and data center expansion**—all in a map‑based UI. In other words, it identifies the deal _and_ ties it to the true actor. [acres.com](https://www.acres.com/data-center-index)
    

> **“Real‑time” vs reality:** Acres markets the Index as revealing activity **months before public announcements** (which often arrive long after land control is secured). In practice the detection window is “as fast as counties record and Acres ingests/curates,” with monthly and weekly refreshes for key datasets and **monthly** Index reporting. [acres.com+2support.acres.com+2](https://www.acres.com/data-center-index)

### 2) Site‑suitability & market context (the “why it matters” layer)

Beyond “who bought,” the Index layers in siting drivers:

- **Power grid**: U.S. **transmission lines** (with voltage, type, status) and **substations** (with type, status, **capacity**, ownership). [landvalues.acres.com+1](https://landvalues.acres.com/map-highlight-us-transmission-lines-map?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
- **Risk & constraints**: floodplains, topography/slope, soils, zoning, and other environmental/land‑use constraints. [acres.com](https://www.acres.com/data-center-index)
    
- **Connectivity**: Acres’ guidance includes **fiber/network overlays** as part of data‑center siting checklists in its site‑selection series. [landvalues.acres.com](https://landvalues.acres.com/part-5-avoiding-costly-pitfalls-data-center-site-selection?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    

Put together, the Index lets you **watch acquisition patterns** (who is land‑banking where) and overlay **power/zoning/risk** to forecast where viable data center capacity will emerge—**often months to years before permits or press**.


# Example timeline: how an AWS‑style purchase gets surfaced

1. **Recording at the county:** a shell LLC purchases 400–800 acres near a **230–345 kV** substation. The deed and any associated mortgage record are recorded.
    
2. **Acres ingestion:** the sale hits Acres as a **courthouse sale** (monthly cycle) and may also appear in the **curated** stream (weekly). [support.acres.com](https://support.acres.com/migration/filter-comparable-sales-data?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
    
3. **Entity unmasking:** Acres AI links the LLC to a broader corporate network (parcels, transactions, mortgages), attributing the buy to a hyperscaler where confidence is sufficient. [landvalues.acres.com](https://landvalues.acres.com/ai-powered-land-sourcing-diligence)
    
4. **Index flagging:** the deal appears in the **Data Center Index** as a **verified DC‑linked sale**, with accompanying monthly market notes. [acres.com](https://www.acres.com/data-center-index)
    
5. **Siting context:** you overlay **substations/transmission** (capacity/voltage/ownership), flood/slope, zoning, and **fiber** to assess build feasibility and timing—well before public announcement. [landvalues.acres.com+2landvalues.acres.com+2](https://landvalues.acres.com/map-highlight-us-power-substations?utm_source=chatgpt.com)
