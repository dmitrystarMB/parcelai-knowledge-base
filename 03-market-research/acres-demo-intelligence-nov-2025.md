# Acres.com Demo Intelligence

**Date**: November 2025
**Presenter**: Colby Burke (Sales), Carter Malloy (Co-founder/Exec)
**Client**: PNK Group (Dmitry Starodubtsev, Aleksei Posokhov) + Alexey Dubov (Advisor)
**Threat Level**: 🔴 Critical

## Executive Summary

Acres.com demonstrated a mature, comprehensive land intelligence platform with significant competitive moats. **Critical finding**: They have 4 table-stakes features we're missing (entity unmasking, power grid data, curated comps, comprehensive transaction database) that are **actively blocking PNK Group's expressed needs**.

**Key Takeaway**: We cannot compete credibly without LLC unmasking, power infrastructure data, and curated transaction comps. These are P0 gaps.

---

## Top Features Demonstrated

1. **LLC Unmasking & Entity Resolution**: AI-powered system revealing beneficial owners behind shell entities controlling ~40% of US land parcels
2. **Data Center Intelligence Index**: Tracks hyperscaler acquisitions 18-36 months before public announcements
3. **Power Grid Infrastructure**: 94,000+ substations with voltage/capacity filtering, transmission lines mapped
4. **Curated Transaction Database**: Weekly hand-verified comps + proprietary pricing in 12 non-disclosure states
5. **Retired Power Plant Feature**: Unique capability to filter facilities by planned retirement year for repurposing opportunities
6. **Multi-Layer Search Engine**: Draw custom shapes + filter by infrastructure (power, fiber, gas) + acreage simultaneously
7. **Fiber Connectivity Mapping**: Current and future fiber lines (market-dependent coverage)
8. **Comprehensive Due Diligence Layers**: Environmental (flood, wetlands, soil), zoning, elevation, permits
9. **Home Builder Index**: Track residential developer land banking patterns for growth corridor prediction
10. **Asset Intelligence ("Spider Web")**: Click any entity → see all 219 parcels, 44 purchases, mapped relationships

## Critical Gaps (P0) 🔴

### 1. **LLC Unmasking / Entity Resolution**
**Why must build**: 40% of US land held through opaque LLCs. PNK asked specifically about "knowing who really owns" and "hidden LLCs". Without this, we can't provide competitive intelligence on hyperscaler land banking.

**Acres capability**:
- Tracks "few dozen" hyperscalers and major data center players
- Maps all associated LLCs back to parent companies (Meta, Amazon, Equinix)
- Real-time acquisition activity monitoring
- "Spider web" feature shows all 219 parcels + 44 purchases for single entity

**Our status**: ❌ Don't have. Module 5 (Affiliated Owners) planned but not same capability.

**PNK validation**: Dmitry specifically interested in "enterprise plan add-ons" for LLC unmasking. Direct need.

---

### 2. **Power Grid Infrastructure Data**
**Why must build**: **Critical for data center site selection**. Gigawatt-scale facilities require 230-345 kV transmission lines. PNK said power/fiber are "definitely invaluable".

**Acres capability**:
- 94,000+ substations across US
- Voltage filtering (230V+, 345V+ for data centers)
- Transmission line mapping
- Capacity, status, ownership details
- Reduces 94,000 results → 10,000 relevant substations with one filter

**Our status**: ❌ Don't have. Planned in Module 1 enhancements but not built.

**PNK validation**: Called power lines "definitely invaluable". Primary use case is data centers.

---

### 3. **Curated Transaction Database**
**Why must build**: **Accurate pricing critical for $10M-$100M+ land deals**. PNK workflow involves underwriting hundreds of parcels.

**Acres capability**:
- Weekly hand-verified comps (staff-curated)
- Monthly courthouse sales (automated)
- **Proprietary pricing in 12 non-disclosure states** (including parts of Pennsylvania - PNK's primary market)
- Data center-specific comparable sales
- "Don't want to pay more than competitors" - exact use case addressed

**Our status**: ⚠️ Public records only. No curation, no non-disclosure state coverage.

**PNK validation**: Pennsylvania is non-disclosure state. Acres showed only 2 transactions found there - gap acknowledged but they have some coverage.

## Key Learnings

### 1. About Market
- Data center development is hot: "Ton of activity" in Ohio, Pennsylvania, Georgia, Florida
- Power availability is #1 constraint for gigawatt-scale facilities
- Retired power plant repurposing is emerging trend: "Big players going around using infrastructure from retired power plants"
- Hyperscalers buy land 18-36 months before announcements through shell LLCs

### 2. About Customer Needs (PNK)
- **Primary market**: Pennsylvania (PJM/PPL power connections)
- **Secondary markets**: Georgia, Florida
- **Current state**: Only 2 sites under active data center investigation
- **Tool gap**: Using "open-source data and broker relationships" - no sophisticated tools
- **Budget signal**: Evaluating $50K-$150K+ enterprise software (asked about pricing)
- **Scale**: "Hundreds of parcels" for pre-screening

### 3. About Technical Approach
- LLC unmasking requires: AI/ML + manual curation + corporate registry data (50 states)
- Power grid data: Public sources available (EIA, HIFLD) but requires processing
- Curated comps: Need in-house research team + county relationships (Acres has "really big data team")
- Non-disclosure states: Requires broker relationships, title company partnerships - 5+ year moat
- Update cadence: Monthly for most data, weekly for curated comps (not real-time)
