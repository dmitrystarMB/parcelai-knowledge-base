## Challenges & Resolutions (Q4)

### Challenge 1: Net Acreage Performance Bottleneck
**Problem:** Calculation struggled with large datasets (6,217 parcels took 30 min)
**Impact:** Could not scale to full database (280K+ parcels)
**Resolution:** Major optimization effort (November)
**Result:** ✅ 45x performance improvement, full dataset processing enabled

### Challenge 2: Zoning Coverage Gaps
**Problem:** "Planned Development" zoning missing from whitelist
**Impact:** Hillsborough County only 45,402 parcels (significantly under-representing market)
**Resolution:** Added Planned Development zoning, reprocessed county
**Result:** ✅ 281,387 parcels (6.2x increase), unlocked massive inventory

### Challenge 3: Contact Data Quality
**Problem:** Inconsistent contact accuracy across sources
**Mitigation:** Feedback system (September), verification spreadsheet (November)
**Status:** 🚧 Ongoing improvement process
**Next Steps:** Integrate ZoomInfo/Spokeo data quality improvements
