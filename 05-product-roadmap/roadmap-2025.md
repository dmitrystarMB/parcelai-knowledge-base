# Product Roadmap 2025

## Overview

Strategic product roadmap for Parcel AI covering 2025 development priorities, organized by quarter and theme.

**Last Updated:** November 2024  
**Planning Horizon:** 2025 Annual  
**Review Cycle:** Monthly

---

## 2025 Strategic Themes

### 1. Anchor Client Success & Validation
Ensure PNK Group and early customers achieve significant value and become referenceable

### 2. Core Platform Maturity
Strengthen foundational capabilities for enterprise reliability and scale

### 3. Market Expansion Readiness
Build features and capabilities needed to scale to more customers

### 4. Data Quality & Coverage
Expand geographic coverage and improve data accuracy

---

## Risk & Mitigation

### Key Risks
1. **Customer retention below targets**
   - Mitigation: Deep customer success focus, rapid issue resolution

2. **Feature delivery delays**
   - Mitigation: Agile sprints, clear prioritization, buffer time

3. **Data quality issues**
   - Mitigation: Quality assurance processes, customer feedback loops

4. **Competition intensifies**
   - Mitigation: Speed to market, customer lock-in, differentiation

## Roadmap Principles

### 1. Customer-Driven
Build what customers need and will pay for

### 2. Iterative & Agile
Ship fast, learn, iterate

### 3. Quality Over Quantity
Better to ship less that works well

### 4. Platform Thinking
Build for extensibility and scale

### 5. Data-Informed
Use metrics to validate decisions
