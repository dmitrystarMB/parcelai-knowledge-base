# Product Roadmap 2025

## Overview

Strategic product roadmap for Parcel AI covering 2024-2026 development priorities, organized by module and timeline. ParcelAI's development follows a modular approach with five core modules designed to transform land acquisition workflows.

**Last Updated:** January 2025
**Planning Horizon:** 2024-2026
**Review Cycle:** Monthly

---

## Module-Based Development Approach

ParcelAI is built around five integrated modules, each addressing a critical pain point in the land acquisition process:

### Module 1: Project Management
**Status:** Launched 2024
**Timeline:** 2 months (Planned: 4 months)

Unified workspace eliminating the need to switch between multiple tools for parcel analysis and project management.

**Key Features:**
- Data integration (Nearmap, Regrid, Environmental, Infrastructure)
- Advanced filtering and parcel categorization
- Project management and presentation tools
- Team collaboration features

**Success Metrics Achieved:**
- Parcel analysis time reduced from 2 hours to 15 minutes (87% reduction)
- 100% team adoption
- 50+ parcels processed through system

---

### Module 2: Owner Contact Search
**Status:** Launched 2024
**Timeline:** 3 months (Planned: 3 months)

Automated search for land owner contact information to enable direct outreach, bypassing brokers and their inflated pricing.

**Key Features:**
- Automated contact discovery (individual and corporate)
- Owner profile management and data enrichment
- Contact organization and feedback loop
- Integration with Peopledatalab and Apollo APIs

**Success Metrics Achieved:**
- 90% success rate finding current owner contacts
- Contact search time reduced from 4 hours to 15 minutes (94% reduction)
- 30+ hours per week of work time freed

---

### Module 3: Automatic Land Qualification
**Status:** Planned - Development Starting Q1 2025
**Timeline:** 8 months
**Target Launch:** Q3 2025

Automate preliminary assessment of land parcels using desktop audit from public sources, without requiring physical site visits.

**Planned Features:**
- AI chat assistant for parcel qualification
- Topography and geotechnical analysis
- Wetlands, flood zones, and environmental risk assessment
- Highway access and infrastructure analysis (fiber, power)
- Development cost estimation and Go/NoGo scoring

**Target Success Metrics:**
- 70% automatic filtering of unsuitable parcels
- Over 70% accuracy of preliminary assessment
- Significant time and cost savings on due diligence

---

### Module 4: Market Signals Monitoring
**Status:** Planned - Development Starting Q1 2025
**Timeline:** 6 months
**Target Launch:** Q2 2025

Early detection system for parcels with high arbitrage potential before they appear on the open market.

**Planned Features:**
- Public hearings and permit documentation monitoring
- Industrial bankruptcy tracking
- Zoning change application monitoring
- LandID integration for encumbrance analysis
- High potential parcel alerts and arbitrage scoring

**Target Success Metrics:**
- 25+ off-market opportunities identified per month
- 30-60 day lead time ahead of competitors
- 10+ high arbitrage potential parcels identified monthly

---

### Module 5: Affiliated Owners Analysis
**Status:** Planned - Development Starting Q1 2025
**Timeline:** 4 months
**Target Launch:** Q2 2025

Identify all parcels owned by affiliated owner groups through corporate relationship and ownership structure analysis.

**Planned Features:**
- KYC/KYB module for comprehensive verification
- Corporate structure and beneficial owner identification
- OSINT relationship mapping
- Automated parcel discovery across ownership networks
- Interactive ownership network visualization

**Target Success Metrics:**
- Identify all additional parcels for each verified owner
- Map complete ownership networks
- Increase deal pipeline through network discoveries
