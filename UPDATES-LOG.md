## 🔍 Research & Analysis Completed

### CoStar Investigation (November 2025)
**Findings:**
- ✅ Leader in commercial building data
- ⚠️ Minimal raw land coverage
- ⚠️ Average exposure ~900 days (range: 25-2,500 days)
- ⚠️ No open API; manual export only; automation complex
- **Conclusion:** Use as supplementary validator, not primary source

### AI Copilot Hypothesis (November 2025)
**Concept:** Natural language query → Automatic enriched parcel analysis
**Example:** "Find industrial parcels 20-50 acres in Dauphin County, PA, net acreage >90% of gross"
**Architecture:** Multi-agent system with automated data enrichment
**Status:** Prototype/hypothesis stage
**Strategic Value:** Future direction of product
