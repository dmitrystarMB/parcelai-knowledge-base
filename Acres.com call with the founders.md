## Key Product Features Demonstrated

### 1. **Data Center Intelligence Index** (Core Differentiator)

**LLC Unmasking Capability:**

- Tracks "few dozen" hyperscalers and major data center players
- Monitors all LLCs and entities associated with each company
- Maps hidden LLCs back to parent companies (Meta, Amazon, Equinix, etc.)
- Real-time acquisition activity monitoring

**Visual Intelligence:**

- Red outlines = current owned parcels
- Points = new transactions over time horizon
- Status taxonomy: Active, Planned, Under Construction, Land Banked

**Status Definitions (clarified in call):**

- **Active:** Site is built, open, serving customers
- **Under Construction:** Ground broken, construction in progress
- **Planned:** Pre-construction, permits in process
- **Land Banked:** Longest-dated options, strategic land holdings

**Value Proposition:**

> "Market and comp intel to showcase where other folks are currently, where they've purchased land for new sites, and help with due diligence and underwriting to understand what kind of impact would they have on us from a power perspective, gaining customers perspective, and even hiring folks at the data center location"

### 2. **Transaction Intelligence**

**Two Data Categories:**

1. **Stealth Land Buys** - No announcements, no power application rumors yet
2. **Verified Land Buys** - Confirmed after trade closes

**Example Showcased:**

- Meta Q3 2024 Iowa transaction - "planting flag in new market" (caught before public announcement)

**Data Points Captured:**

- Transaction amount
- Transaction date
- Total computed acres
- County, State
- Buyer name (hidden LLC)
- Parent company tag
- Location mapping

**Competitive Intelligence Use Case:**

> "I'm sure you guys have seen there's a ton of 'Meta's coming to town' rumors, or 'Meta XYZ applied for power' - we bring back those confirmed land buys once the trade closes"

**Non-Disclosure States Challenge:**

- 13 non-disclosure states in US
- Pennsylvania example: Only 2 transactions found (Amazon 5/27/2025, Databank 2019)
- Data "definitely richer" in disclosure states but some coverage everywhere

### 3. **Data Center-Specific Sales Comps**

Critical pain point addressed:

> "I have a lot of folks going out acquiring land for data centers - they're okay paying more than typical price per acre, but they don't want to pay more than their competitors"

**Workflow:**

- Jump into transactions dataset
- Search by state and county
- Get "accurate and timely data center specific sales comps"
- Critical for underwriting process

### 4. **Asset Intelligence / Competitor Deep Dive**

**Spider Web Feature:**

- Click on any entity (e.g., Amazon Data Services Inc.)
- Pull up all properties and assets tied to that entity
- Example shown: 219 parcels owned, 44 purchases
- Reverse lookup by owner name
- Visualize all parcels on map

**Coverage Documentation:**

- Comprehensive list of tracked hyperscalers available
- Data dictionary provided
- Full transparency on what companies are monitored

### 5. **Power & Energy Infrastructure** (Critical for Data Centers)

**Substations & Transmission Lines:**

- 94,000+ substations in database across US
- Filterable by voltage threshold
- Example: Filter for 230V+ and 345V+ (data center requirements)
- Reduces dataset from 94,000 to ~10,000 results
- Identifies "higher voltage, higher capacity substations and transmission lines set up for data centers"

**Electric Retail Service Providers:**

- Know who you're working with in any market
- FPL NextEra Energy example mentioned (based in Juno Beach, FL)

**Power Plants Intelligence:**

- All power plants visualized across US
- Sortable by power type
- **Retired Power Plants Feature** - unique capability:
    - Filter by planned retirement year
    - Use case: Repurposing retired facilities infrastructure
    - Example: Filter "past 5 years" retirements
    - Reduces 3,500 results to 684 "fresher retirements"
    - Market trend: "Big players going around using infrastructure from retired power plants or cozying up next door to them"

**Natural Gas Infrastructure:**

- Gas lines overlay available
- Important for many customers

### 6. **Fiber Connectivity** (Critical for AI Data Centers)

**Coverage:**

- Current fiber lines
- Future fiber lines
- **Market dependent** - some markets have more data than others

**Example Shown - Ohio Market:**

- New Albany area (heavy data center activity)
- Grove City fiber (purple visualization)
- Gahana future fiber
- Dublin fiber
- Visual correlation: "Data centers are just peppered along the fiber lines"

**Insight from visualization:**

> "The story tells itself where the data centers are just lying completely on the fiber, natural gas, and those higher capacity higher voltage transmission lines and substations"

### 7. **Parcel-Level Due Diligence**

**Environmental & Physical:**

- Property tax information
- Last trade details
- Elevation with adjustable contour levels
- Flood and wetland risk (toggleable layers)
- Soil types with map location highlighting
- Land cover information

**Zoning Intelligence:**

- Overlay zoning for any market
- Parcel-level zoning codes (example: "RA" zone shown)
- Filter by zoning type
- Critical challenge identified: "Allowance for data center development stated really different from state to state... updates constantly, changing everything every month"
- PNK workflow: "Go through every county, township, ordinance map to look and understand if it's really allowed to go for the data center"

**Building Permits:**

- All in system
- Available for review

### 8. **Parcel Search & Filtering Engine**

**Demonstrated Workflow:**

1. Visualize all substations and transmission lines across US
2. Filter for 230V+ voltage (94,000 → 10,000+ results)
3. Run same filter on substations
4. Select target market (Georgia shown)
5. Draw custom shape on map around power infrastructure cluster
6. Add acreage filter (100-200 acres example)
7. Returns 110 properties matching all criteria

**Flexibility:**

- Search by state/county
- Custom shape drawing
- Multiple filter layers simultaneously
- Can add additional data overlays during search (natural gas, etc.)

### 9. **Home Builder Index** (Secondary Product)

**Tracking:**

- All major home builders
- Land acquisitions for subdivisions and developments
- Growth pathways around cities
- Visualized outside and around each US city

**Use Case for Industrial/Logistics:**

> "Some folks in the industrial logistics space use that for their network and capacity planning, just based on kind of where developments and where people are going"

**Industrial Warehouse Tracking:**

- NOT currently tracked for transactions
- Willing to build custom datasets if client engages

**Other Tracking:**

- Retail around the world
- Directly owned distribution centers

### 10. **AI-Powered Features** (Newer Addition)

**Contextual Intelligence:**

> "Another great piece of this AI update is we'll bubble up any relevant information"

- Automatically surfaces relevant data layers for site selection
- Helps with de-risking and due diligence process

## Conclusion

Acres.com is a formidable, well-established competitor with significant data moats and strong domain expertise. However, they have clear limitations:

- 30-day update lag
- US-only coverage
- Enterprise-only positioning
- Limited vertical coverage
- Market-dependent fiber data
- Acknowledged zoning challenges

Parcel AI can win by being **faster** (real-time), **smarter** (AI-powered), **broader** (multi-vertical, global), and **more accessible** (self-serve + enterprise).

The key is not to compete head-to-head on historical US data depth, but to leapfrog with modern technology, better UX, and a product-led growth model that scales beyond just enterprise sales.

**Critical Success Factor:** Launch with enough parity features (LLC unmasking, transactions, power infrastructure) that you're credible, plus 2-3 clear differentiators (real-time alerts, AI scoring, self-serve) that make you preferable for a segment of the market. Then expand from there.
