## Overview

This document outlines the framework and process for prioritizing features and product investments.

**Last Updated:** November 2024

---

## Prioritization Framework

### RICE Scoring

**Components:**
- **Reach:** How many users/customers will this impact?
- **Impact:** How much will it impact them? (0.25 = minimal, 3 = massive)
- **Confidence:** How confident are we in our estimates? (50%-100%)
- **Effort:** How much time will it take? (person-months)

**Formula:** RICE Score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) / Effort

---

## Prioritization Criteria

### Strategic Alignment (Weight: 30%)
- Aligns with product vision
- Supports business objectives
- Advances competitive position
- Enables market expansion

### Customer Value (Weight: 25%)
- Solves significant pain point
- Requested by multiple customers
- Impacts retention or expansion
- Drives user adoption

### Business Impact (Weight: 25%)
- Revenue potential
- Cost savings
- Market differentiation
- Risk mitigation

### Feasibility (Weight: 20%)
- Technical complexity
- Resource requirements
- Dependencies
- Time to market

---

## Feature Categories

### Must Have (P0)
- Critical for core functionality
- Blocker for customer success
- Competitive requirement
- Regulatory/compliance need

### Should Have (P1)
- Strong customer demand
- Significant value creation
- Reasonable effort
- Clear ROI

### Nice to Have (P2)
- Incremental improvement
- Limited demand
- Lower business impact
- Future consideration

### Won't Have (Declined)
- Doesn't align with strategy
- Too complex for value
- Better alternatives exist
- Out of scope
