## Business Requirements

### Key Business Objectives

1. **Deploy $200M Capital Efficiently**
   - **Description:** Successfully deploy $200M in capital for US land acquisition without broker intermediaries
   - **Success Metrics:**
     - Direct owner contact success rate: 90%
     - Cost savings vs. broker-mediated deals
     - Number of off-market deals secured
   - **Timeline:** Ongoing (2025-2026)
   - **Priority:** High

2. **Find Land for Two Product Types**
   - **Description:** Identify and qualify parcels suitable for logistics terminals/industrial parks AND AI data centers
   - **Success Metrics:**
     - Active pipeline of qualified parcels for both product types
     - 70% automatic filtering of unsuitable parcels
     - Minimum 50+ parcels processed through system
   - **Timeline:** Ongoing
   - **Priority:** High

3. **Accelerate Land Acquisition Process**
   - **Description:** Reduce time spent on parcel research and owner contact discovery
   - **Success Metrics:**
     - Parcel analysis: <15 minutes (from 2 hours)
     - Owner contact search: <15 minutes (from 4 hours)
     - 30+ hours/week time savings for team
   - **Timeline:** Achieved with Modules 1-2, enhance with Modules 3-5
   - **Priority:** High

4. **Access Off-Market Opportunities**
   - **Description:** Identify and secure parcels before they hit the open market
   - **Success Metrics:**
     - 25+ off-market opportunities per month
     - 30-90 days ahead of competitors
     - Access to affiliated owner parcels
   - **Timeline:** Q1-Q3 2025 (Modules 4-5)
   - **Priority:** High

## Requirements Revealed from Acres.com Demo (November 2025)

**Source**: Product demo with Acres.com observed by Parcel AI team
**Date**: November 2025
**Validation**: PNK Group directly asked about / validated need for these capabilities

### REQ-DEMO-001: Power Grid Infrastructure Intelligence

**Priority**: 🔴 P0 CRITICAL
**Status**: Not Started
**Target Date**: Q1 2026

**Description**:
Comprehensive US power grid data including substations (voltage, capacity, ownership), transmission lines, and power plants with retirement schedules. Critical for gigawatt-scale data center site selection.

**Source Question/Validation**:
- PNK reaction: **"Power lines definitely invaluable"** (direct quote validating criticality)
- Use case: Filter 230V+ and 345V+ substations for data center power requirements
- Workflow need: Start infrastructure-led search with power grid, then find suitable parcels

**What PNK Saw in Acres Demo**:
- 94,000+ US substations with voltage/capacity/ownership details
- Transmission line mapping
- Voltage filtering (94K→10K substations with 230V+ filter)
- Retired power plant feature (unique): Filter 3,500 plants by retirement year → 684 "fresher retirements" for repurposing

**Business Impact**:
- **Blocking**: Cannot perform data center site selection without power infrastructure mapping
- **PNK primary use case**: Data centers require 230-345 kV proximity
- **Competitive disadvantage**: Acres has this as table stakes; we don't

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] 230+ kV substation coverage for all target markets (PA, GA, FL)
- [ ] Voltage filtering capability (230V+, 345V+)
- [ ] Transmission line mapping with specifications
- [ ] Power plant data including retirement schedules
- [ ] Map-based visualization with infrastructure overlays
- [ ] Accuracy >95% for location and voltage data

**Dependencies**:
- Data sources: EIA, HIFLD, public utility filings
- GIS processing pipeline
- Map visualization integration

**Notes**:
- Public data sources available (EIA-860, EIA-411, HIFLD)
- M effort (2-3 months) with existing public data
- Retired power plant feature is differentiator Acres has

---

### REQ-DEMO-002: LLC Unmasking & Entity Resolution

**Priority**: 🔴 P0 CRITICAL
**Status**: Not Started
**Target Date**: Q2 2026

**Description**:
AI-powered system to reveal beneficial owners behind shell entities (LLCs, trusts, corporations) controlling land parcels. Track hyperscaler and major player land acquisitions 18-36 months before public announcements.

**Source Question/Validation**:
- PNK question: "What exact companies are tracked in the Data Center Index?"
- PNK interest: Asked about "enterprise plan add-ons" for LLC tracking
- Use case: Understand competitive land banking by Amazon, Meta, Google, Microsoft

**What PNK Saw in Acres Demo**:
- "Spider web" feature: Click Amazon Data Services Inc. → revealed 219 parcels owned, 44 purchases
- Meta Q3 2024 Iowa transaction caught before public announcement
- Entity attribution mapping (LLC → parent company)
- Status tracking: Active, Under Construction, Planned, Land Banked
- Tracks "few dozen" hyperscalers and major data center players

**Business Impact**:
- **Blocking**: Cannot provide competitive intelligence on hyperscaler land banking
- **Strategic value**: Understand where competitors are acquiring land 18-36 months early
- **PNK validation**: Specifically asked for coverage document showing which companies tracked

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] Successfully unmask 30%+ of shell entities in target markets
- [ ] Track major hyperscalers (Amazon, Meta, Google, Microsoft, etc.)
- [ ] Confidence scoring >80% for automated connections
- [ ] Reverse lookup: Click entity → see all parcels owned
- [ ] Transaction attribution (which LLC purchased, parent company identified)
- [ ] Coverage documentation showing tracked entities

**Dependencies**:
- AI/ML entity resolution algorithms
- Corporate registry data partnerships (50 states)
- Graph database for relationship mapping
- Manual verification process (human-in-the-loop like Acres)

**Notes**:
- 40% of US land held through LLCs (major market reality)
- XL effort (6-9 months) - complex AI + data partnerships
- Acres has 5-year moat with entity resolution
- Module 5 (Affiliated Owners) addresses some of this but needs expansion

### REQ-DEMO-003: Fiber Connectivity Infrastructure

**Priority**: 🟡 P1 HIGH
**Status**: Not Started (Fiberlocator integration planned)
**Target Date**: Q2 2026

**Description**:
Mapping of current and planned fiber optic lines for connectivity analysis. Critical for AI data center site selection requiring low-latency, high-bandwidth connections.

**Source Question/Validation**:
- PNK reaction: **"Fiber definitely invaluable"** (direct quote validating criticality)
- Use case: AI data centers require high-bandwidth fiber connectivity
- Pattern observed: "Data centers are just lying completely on the fiber"

**What PNK Saw in Acres Demo**:
- Current fiber lines mapped
- Future/planned fiber lines shown (different visualization)
- Ohio market example: New Albany, Grove City, Gahana, Dublin fiber with data centers "peppered along the fiber lines"
- Visual correlation with power infrastructure
- **Limitation acknowledged**: "Market dependent - some markets have more data than others"

**Business Impact**:
- **Important but not blocking**: Data centers need fiber but power is higher priority
- **Validation**: PNK called it "definitely invaluable"
- **Opportunity**: Acres coverage is market-dependent (acknowledged weakness)

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] Current fiber lines mapped for target markets (PA, GA, FL)
- [ ] Future/planned fiber build-outs shown
- [ ] Provider identification (which telecom serves area)
- [ ] Visual overlay with power infrastructure
- [ ] More comprehensive coverage than Acres' "market-dependent" approach

**Dependencies**:
- Fiberlocator partnership (already identified in Module 1 enhancements)
- Data licensing agreements
- Map visualization integration

**Notes**:
- M effort (2-3 months) depending on data partnership
- Acres acknowledged limitation - opportunity for us to be better
- Module 1 enhancement already mentions Fiberlocator

### REQ-DEMO-004: Curated Transaction Database with Non-Disclosure State Coverage

**Priority**: 🔴 P0 CRITICAL
**Status**: Not Started
**Target Date**: Q2 2026

**Description**:
Weekly hand-verified comparable sales database supplementing automated courthouse sales, with proprietary transaction prices in non-disclosure states (especially Pennsylvania - PNK's primary market).

**Source Question/Validation**:
- PNK question: **"What about Pennsylvania specifically? It's a non-disclosure state."**
- Critical concern: Pennsylvania is **PNK's PRIMARY market** (PJM/PPL power connections)
- Use case: "Don't want to pay more than competitors" for land deals

**What PNK Saw in Acres Demo**:
- Monthly automated courthouse sales (3,000+ counties)
- **Weekly hand-verified comps** by in-house research team
- **Proprietary pricing in 12 non-disclosure states** (5-year moat)
- Pennsylvania example: Only 2 transactions found (Amazon 5/27/2025, Databank 2019)
- Acres acknowledged: "Data definitely richer in disclosure states but some coverage everywhere"
- Investment disclosed: "Tens of millions of dollars" and "really big data team" for curation

**Business Impact**:
- **Critical for PNK**: Pennsylvania is their primary market (non-disclosure state)
- **Underwriting requirement**: Accurate comps essential for $10M-$100M+ land deals
- **Acres moat**: 5-year head start with county relationships and curation team

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] Weekly update cadence for target markets (PA, GA, FL initially)
- [ ] 95%+ accuracy for verified transactions
- [ ] Coverage of Pennsylvania (non-disclosure state) with alternative data sources
- [ ] In-house research analyst (1-2 FTE) for manual curation
- [ ] Catch and correct 10%+ of automated errors
- [ ] Data center-specific filtering for relevant comps

**Dependencies**:
- Research analyst hire (1-2 FTE)
- County recorder relationships (start with PA, GA, FL)
- Broker/title company partnerships for NDS pricing
- Curation workflow tooling

**Notes**:
- L effort (4-6 months + ongoing research team)
- Pennsylvania NDS coverage is make-or-break for PNK
- Cannot fully match Acres' 5-year moat quickly, but can start with PNK's priority markets

### REQ-DEMO-005: Automated Zoning Intelligence & Change Monitoring

**Priority**: 🟡 P1 HIGH
**Status**: Partial (basic zoning planned in Module 3)
**Target Date**: Q3 2026

**Description**:
Deep zoning intelligence with automated monitoring of ordinance changes. PNK manually researches county-by-county zoning for data center allowances - major pain point.

**Source Question/Validation**:
- PNK question: **"What about zoning? We go through every county, township, ordinance map to look and understand if it's really allowed to go for the data center"**
- Major pain point: Manual research across counties is time-consuming
- Acres acknowledged weakness: **"Updates constantly, changing everything every month"** (no strong solution)

**What PNK Saw in Acres Demo**:
- Basic zoning codes shown with parcel overlay (e.g., "RA" zone)
- **Limitation acknowledged**: "Allowance for data center development stated really different from state to state... updates constantly, changing everything every month"
- No deep zoning analysis (setbacks, FAR, height limits, permitted uses)
- No automated change monitoring

**Business Impact**:
- **Significant pain point**: PNK manually researches zoning across many counties
- **Competitive opportunity**: Acres admitted this is hard and changing constantly
- **Differentiation**: Automated monitoring could beat Acres' manual approach

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] Complete zoning code analysis (setbacks, height limits, lot coverage, permitted uses, FAR)
- [ ] Data center use allowance by jurisdiction
- [ ] **Automated ordinance change monitoring** (email alerts when zoning updated)
- [ ] AHJ (Authorities Having Jurisdiction) contact database with phone numbers
- [ ] Coverage of 100+ municipalities (prioritize PA, GA, FL)
- [ ] Zoning data accuracy >95%

**Dependencies**:
- Municipal zoning database integrations
- Ordinance parsing (AI/manual hybrid)
- Change detection system
- AHJ contact curation

**Notes**:
- M effort (3-4 months) if prioritized
- Module 3 already plans basic zoning
- **Opportunity vs. Acres**: They acknowledged difficulty - we can differentiate
- Could partner with Dodda.ai for comprehensive zoning data

### REQ-DEMO-006: Portfolio-Scale Pre-Due Diligence Automation

**Priority**: ✅ P0 CRITICAL (Already Planned - Module 3)
**Status**: Planned for Q3 2025
**Target Date**: Q3 2025

**Description**:
Automated preliminary land parcel assessment for hundreds of parcels using desktop audit. Enables Go/NoGo decisions before expensive engineering studies and site visits.

**Source Question/Validation**:
- PNK question: **"Can you handle hundreds of parcels for pre-due diligence?"**
- Use case: PNK has "hundreds of parcels" in pipeline needing systematic screening
- Value prop: "Maybe you would have sent 10 sites to feasibility study... with Acres you actually disqualify three and only send seven... end up saving money"

**What PNK Saw in Acres Demo**:
- Environmental layers: Flood zones, wetlands, soil types
- Topography: Elevation with adjustable contours
- Zoning overlays (basic)
- Property tax, transaction history, building permits
- Manual white-glove service: "Send us 3-5 addresses, we'll prepare detailed analysis, ~2 week period"

**Business Impact**:
- **Validation**: PNK specifically asked about handling hundreds of parcels
- **Our advantage**: Module 3 designed for **automation** vs. Acres' manual service
- **Time savings**: Filter 70% of unsuitable parcels automatically

**Acceptance Criteria**:
- [ ] All Module 3 planned features (flood, wetlands, soil, topography, environmental, traffic, FAR, scoring)
- [ ] **Batch processing**: Handle hundreds of parcels simultaneously (vs. Acres' manual 3-5 at a time)
- [ ] **Automated scoring**: Go/NoGo for logistics and data center use cases
- [ ] Preliminary assessment accuracy >70%
- [ ] Time: 2-3 minutes per parcel automated analysis

**Dependencies**:
- Module 3 development (already planned Q3 2025)
- Environmental data integrations
- AI scoring infrastructure

**Notes**:
- Already planned in Module 3 - **competitive advantage vs. Acres' manual approach**
- Acres does this manually with white-glove service
- **Our differentiation**: Automation at scale
