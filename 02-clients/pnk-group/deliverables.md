# PNK Group - Deliverables

## Overview

This document tracks all deliverables, milestones, and commitments made to PNK Group. It serves as a project tracker for client-specific work and ensures accountability.

**Last Updated:** November 2024

---

## Product Modules Overview

ParcelAI delivers 5 specialized modules to PNK Group:
- **Module 1:** Project Management (MVP Launched - Active Use)
- **Module 2:** Owner Contact Search (MVP Launched - Active Use)
- **Module 3:** Automatic Land Qualification (Planned - 8 months)
- **Module 4:** Market Signals Monitoring (Planned - 6 months)
- **Module 5:** Affiliated Owners Analysis (Planned - 4 months)

All modules work in synergy: project management serves as the foundation, owner search and affiliation analysis expand the pipeline, market signal monitoring finds hidden opportunities, and automatic qualification filters only promising parcels for investment.

---

## Completed Deliverables (2024)

### DEL-001: Module 1 - Project Management
**Type:** Feature / Platform Module
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Completed & In Active Use

**Description:**
Unified workspace for parcel analysis integrating multiple data sources (Nearmap, Regrid, wetlands, flood zones, carrier hotels, AI data centers). Enables team collaboration, project management, and parcel categorization for logistics vs. data centers.

**Business Value:**
Team saves 3-4 hours per day on routine operations, all parcel data centralized in one location.

**Success Metrics Achieved:**
- ✓ Reduced parcel analysis time from 2 hours to 15 minutes
- ✓ 100% of team using platform
- ✓ Minimum 50 parcels processed through system

**Timeline:**
- **Planned:** 4 months
- **Actual:** 2 months (delivered ahead of schedule)
- **Launched:** 2024

**Key Features Delivered:**
- Nearmap integration (detailed drone imagery)
- Regrid integration (cadastral data)
- Wetlands & flood zones data integration
- PNK projects and market deals data integration
- Carrier Hotels and AI data-centers integration
- Parcel filtering (area, zoning, owners, etc.)
- Parcel categorization by use type (logistics vs. data centers)
- Project creation and management
- "Palette" functionality for stakeholder presentations
- Team collaboration (comments, notes, deal statuses)
- Feedback form for owner contacts
- Map markers for project visualization

### DEL-002: Module 2 - Owner Contact Search
**Type:** Feature / Platform Module
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Completed & In Active Use

**Description:**
Automated search for land owner contact information (individuals and companies) enabling direct outreach to owners bypassing brokers.

**Business Value:**
Team saves 2-3 hours per deal on owner contact discovery. With 10 parcels/week, this frees up 30 hours of work time weekly.

**Success Metrics Achieved:**
- ✓ 90% success rate finding current owner contacts
- ✓ Reduced contact search time from 4 hours to 15 minutes

**Timeline:**
- **Planned:** 3 months
- **Actual:** 3 months (on schedule)
- **Launched:** 2024

**Key Features Delivered:**
- Automatic contact search for individual owners
- Automatic contact search for corporate owners
- Owner profile creation with complete contact information
- Automatic data enrichment (email, phone, LinkedIn)
- Communications tracking (planned for future enhancement)

## Active Deliverables

### DEL-003: Module 3 - Automatic Land Qualification
**Type:** Feature / Platform Module
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Planned - Development Starting Q1 2025

**Description:**
Automated preliminary assessment of land parcels using desktop audit from public sources, without physical site visits or lengthy manual audits.

**Business Value:**
Focus only on parcels with real potential, avoiding wasted resources on unsuitable options.

**Target Success Metrics:**
- Automatic filtering of 70% of unsuitable parcels
- Preliminary assessment accuracy >70%
- Time and budget savings on each rejected parcel

**Timeline:**
- **Estimated Duration:** 8 months
- **Target Launch:** Q3 2025

**Planned Features:**
- AI chat assistant for land qualification questions
- Underground object analysis (mines)
- Automatic topography verification
- Automatic geotech parameters check
- Automatic wetlands & flood zones verification (wetland report)
- Automatic environmental risks check (environmental report)
- Automatic traffic impact analysis (traffic report)
- Highway access analysis
- Fiber network proximity analysis
- Electrical grid capacity analysis
- Preliminary development cost estimation
- Automatic FAR (Floor Area Ratio) verification

**Dependencies:**
- Modules 1 & 2 foundation
- Integration with additional data sources

---

### DEL-004: Module 4 - Market Signals Monitoring
**Type:** Feature / Platform Module
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Planned - Development Starting Q1 2025

**Description:**
Early detection system for parcels with high arbitrage potential before they appear on the open market (off-the-market deals).

**Business Value:**
First knowledge of promising parcels, ability to purchase land before price increases after permits or zoning changes.

**Target Success Metrics:**
- Discover 25+ off-market opportunities per month
- Lead competitors by 30-60 days
- Identify 10+ parcels with high arbitrage potential

**Timeline:**
- **Estimated Duration:** 6 months
- **Target Launch:** Q2 2025

**Planned Features:**
- Public hearings and permit documentation parsing
- Industrial company bankruptcy monitoring
- Pre-development meetings and zoning change tracking
- LandID integration for encumbrance analysis
- High arbitrage potential parcel alert system

**Dependencies:**
- Legal/regulatory data sources integration
- Alerting system infrastructure

---

### DEL-005: Module 5 - Affiliated Owners Analysis
**Type:** Feature / Platform Module
**Priority:** High
**Status:** Planned - Development Starting Q1 2025

**Description:**
Identify all parcels owned by affiliated owner groups through corporate relationship and ownership structure analysis.

**Business Value:**
Access to "off the market" parcels not officially for sale, ability to acquire more land through same owners.

**Target Success Metrics:**
- Identify all additional parcels for each verified owner
- Increase potential deal pipeline

**Timeline:**
- **Estimated Duration:** 4 months
- **Target Launch:** Q2 2025

**Planned Features:**
- KYC/KYB (Know Your Customer/Business) module
- Official registry data collection (companies, registration numbers, key personnel)
- Relationship mapping using OSINT tools
- Ultimate Beneficial Owner (UBO) identification
- Automated search for all parcels owned by related companies
- Ownership network visualization

**Dependencies:**
- Corporate data sources integration
- OSINT tools integration
