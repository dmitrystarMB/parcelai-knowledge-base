## Feedback Response Process

### How We're Addressing Feedback

1. **Acknowledged:** All feedback logged and acknowledged within 48 hours
2. **Prioritized:** Reviewed in weekly product meetings
3. **Communicated:** Clients informed of prioritization decisions
4. **Implemented:** Updates included in product roadmap
5. **Closed Loop:** Clients notified when feedback is addressed

### Communication Cadence
- **Weekly:** Internal feedback review
- **Monthly:** Client updates on addressed items
- **Quarterly:** Roadmap updates incorporating feedback
