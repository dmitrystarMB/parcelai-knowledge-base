# Acres.com Product Demo - PNK Group

**Date**: November 2025
**Type**: Product Demo + Q&A
**Presenters**: Colby Burke (Sales, 1+ year at Acres), Carter Malloy (Co-founder/Exec)
**Attendees**: Dmitry Starodubtsev (PNK Group), Aleksei Posokhov (PNK Group), Alexey Dubov (Advisor)

---

## Meeting Summary

Acres.com demonstrated comprehensive land intelligence platform focused on data center site selection. Demo revealed **4 critical gaps** in our platform (LLC unmasking, power grid, curated comps, transaction intelligence) that are actively blocking PNK Group's needs.

**Key Takeaway**: Acres is formidable competitor with 5-year data moats. We must build entity unmasking, power infrastructure data, and curated transaction comps to compete credibly.

**PNK Reaction**: Very positive. Dmitry: "Information is like gold here... you guys did a great job. Really impressive job... great great job." Alek sei: "Really great stuff and some really useful data."

---

## Demo Flow

### Part 1: Data Center Intelligence Index & Entity Unmasking (15 min)

**Features shown**:
- LLC Unmasking: Maps shell entities → parent companies (Meta, Amazon, Equinix)
- Data Center Index: Tracks hyperscaler acquisitions 18-36 months before announcements
- Spider Web: Click entity → see all 219 parcels owned (Amazon example)
- Transaction Intelligence: Stealth vs. verified land buys
- Status tracking: Active, Under Construction, Planned, Land Banked

**Key moments**:
- Showed Meta Q3 2024 Iowa transaction caught before public announcement ("planting flag in new market")
- Demonstrated Amazon Data Services Inc.: 219 parcels owned, 44 purchases
- Pennsylvania example: Only 2 transactions found (Amazon 5/27/2025, Databank 2019) - acknowledged non-disclosure state limitation

**PNK reaction**: Strong interest. Dmitry asked for coverage document to understand "what exact companies tracked". Interested in "enterprise plan add-ons" for LLC tracking.

---

### Part 2: Power Grid & Infrastructure Intelligence (20 min)

**Features shown**:
- 94,000+ US substations with voltage/capacity/ownership
- Transmission line mapping
- Voltage filtering: 230V+, 345V+ for data centers (94K→10K results)
- Retired power plant feature: Filter by planned retirement year
- Natural gas infrastructure overlay
- Fiber connectivity (current + future lines)

**Key moments**:
- Power plant retirements: 3,500 total → filtered "past 5 years" → 684 "fresher retirements" for repurposing
- Ohio fiber example: Showed New Albany, Grove City, Gahana, Dublin fiber with data centers "peppered along the fiber lines"
- FPL NextEra Energy example (Juno Beach, FL) for retail service provider identification
- Market trend: "Big players going around using infrastructure from retired power plants or cozying up next door to them"

**PNK reaction**: **"Power lines definitely invaluable, fiber definitely invaluable"** - direct validation of criticality

---

### Part 3: Multi-Layer Search Workflow Demo (15 min)

**Features shown**:
- Infrastructure-led search: Start with power/fiber, find suitable parcels
- Custom shape drawing on map
- Multi-layer filtering: Geographic + infrastructure + acreage simultaneously
- Georgia example: 110 properties found matching 100-200 acres + 230V+ proximity

**Workflow demonstrated**:
1. Visualize all US substations/transmission lines
2. Filter 230V+ voltage (data center requirement)
3. Select Georgia market
4. Draw custom shape around power infrastructure cluster
5. Add 100-200 acre filter
6. **Result**: 110 properties in ~5 minutes

**Key moments**:
- Instant filtering across 150M parcels
- Visual correlation: "Data centers are just lying completely on the fiber, natural gas, and those higher capacity higher voltage transmission lines and substations"

**PNK reaction**: No direct questions but workflow directly addresses their "hundreds of parcels" screening need

---

### Part 4: Transaction Database & Sales Comps (10 min)

**Features shown**:
- Courthouse sales (monthly automated updates from 3,000+ counties)
- Curated sales (weekly hand-verified by in-house research team)
- Data center-specific comparable sales
- Non-disclosure state coverage: Proprietary pricing in 12 NDS

**Key moments**:
- Pain point addressed: "I have a lot of folks going out acquiring land for data centers - they're okay paying more than typical price per acre, but they don't want to pay more than their competitors"
- Investment disclosed: "Tens of millions of dollars" and "long time" to build county relationships
- Data team: "Really big data team" for curation
- County data quality varies: Some counties still use "physical CD, floppy disk" (!!)

**PNK reaction**: Asked specifically about Pennsylvania (non-disclosure state). Critical concern as Pennsylvania is their primary market.

---

### Part 5: Due Diligence Layers (10 min)

**Features shown**:
- Environmental: Flood zones, wetlands, soil types with map highlighting
- Topography: Elevation with adjustable contour levels
- Zoning: Parcel-level codes with overlay
- Property tax, transaction history, building permits

**Key moments**:
- Zoning challenge acknowledged: "Allowance for data center development stated really different from state to state... updates constantly, changing everything every month"
- Example shown: "RA" zoning code with visual overlay

**PNK reaction**: **Major pain point identified**: "Go through every county, township, ordinance map to look and understand if it's really allowed to go for the data center" - current manual process

---

### Part 6: Home Builder Index (Secondary Product) (5 min)

**Features shown**:
- Tracks all major home builder land acquisitions
- Growth corridor predictions 3-5 years early
- Use case: Industrial/logistics network planning

**Key moments**:
- Industrial warehouse transactions: "NOT currently tracked. Willing to build custom datasets if client engages."

**PNK reaction**: Asked about industrial warehouse tracking (their logistics background). **Opportunity for us** - industrial is OUR positioning.

### Part 7: AI Features & Platform Evolution (5 min)

**Features shown**:
- AI-powered contextual intelligence: "Bubbles up any relevant information"
- Entity unmasking AI (core capability)
- Product velocity: "Been here just over a year... seeing how much the product's improved in that amount of time is crazy"

**PNK reaction**: No specific AI questions

## PNK Q&A Summary

### Critical Questions

**Q1: "What exact companies are tracked in the Data Center Index?"**
- **A**: "Few dozen hyperscalers and major data center players" + coverage document to be provided
- **Reveals**: PNK needs competitive intelligence on specific players for market strategy
- **Our gap**: ❌ No entity tracking

**Q2: "What about Pennsylvania specifically? It's a non-disclosure state."**
- **A**: "13 non-disclosure states in US. Pennsylvania only 2 transactions found (Amazon 5/27/2025, Databank 2019). Data 'definitely richer' in disclosure states but some coverage everywhere."
- **Reveals**: **PNK's PRIMARY market is Pennsylvania** (PJM/PPL power connections)
- **Our gap**: ❌ No non-disclosure state workaround - critical for PNK's main market

**Q3: "Do you track industrial warehouse transactions?"**
- **A**: "NOT currently tracked for transactions. Willing to build custom datasets if client engages."
- **Reveals**: PNK has mixed-use interest (data centers + industrial/logistics)
- **Our opportunity**: ⭐ Industrial is OUR focus, not Acres' - differentiation opportunity

**Q4: "Can you handle hundreds of parcels for pre-due diligence?"**
- **A**: "Yes, send us 3-5 specific addresses, we'll prepare detailed analysis, ~2 week period for consideration, establish 'report structure' for pipeline management"
- **Reveals**: PNK has large portfolio pipeline needing systematic screening
- **Our strength**: ✅ Module 3 (Land Qualification) designed for this with automation

**Q5: "What about zoning? We go through every county, township, ordinance map..."**
- **A**: "Allowance for data center development stated really different from state to state... updates constantly, changing everything every month" (acknowledged challenge)
- **Reveals**: **Zoning is major pain point** for PNK - manual county-by-county research
- **Our opportunity**: ⭐ Automated zoning monitoring could differentiate vs. Acres' weakness

**Q6: "How does enterprise plan vs. add-on pricing work?"**
- **A**: Base platform + proprietary indices as add-ons (Data Center Index, Home Builder Index)
- **Reveals**: PNK evaluating budget, comfortable with enterprise pricing ($50K-$150K+ likely)
- **Our fit**: ✅ Similar pricing model

**Q7: [Validated by reaction] "Power lines definitely invaluable"**
- **A**: Demonstrated 94,000 substations, voltage filtering, transmission lines
- **Reveals**: **Power infrastructure is #1 priority** for data center site selection
- **Our gap**: ❌ No power grid data - critical gap

**Q8: [Validated by reaction] "Fiber definitely invaluable"**
- **A**: Demonstrated fiber lines in Ohio. Acknowledged "market dependent - some markets have more data than others"
- **Reveals**: **Fiber critical for AI data centers** requiring high bandwidth
- **Our gap**: ❌ No fiber data currently
