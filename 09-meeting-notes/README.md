# Meeting Notes - Parcel AI

## Overview

Папка содержит заметки со встреч, организованные по месяцам.

---

## Structure

Заметки организованы по папкам в формате `YYYY-MM/`:
- `2024-11/` - November 2024
- `2024-12/` - December 2024
- `2025-01/` - January 2025

---

## Naming Convention

**Format**: `YYYY-MM-DD_meeting-type_topic.md`

**Examples**:
- `2025-01-15_client-meeting_pnk-group-kickoff.md`
- `2025-01-16_internal_roadmap-planning.md`
- `2025-01-17_demo_prospect-acme-corp.md`

---

## Meeting Types

- `client-meeting` - Встречи с клиентами
- `prospect-meeting` - Встречи с потенциальными клиентами
- `internal` - Внутренние встречи команды
- `demo` - Демонстрации продукта
- `review` - Review встречи (sprint review, quarterly review)
- `planning` - Планирование (roadmap, sprint planning)
- `retrospective` - Ретроспективы
- `standup` - Daily standups (если записываем)
- `all-hands` - Общие встречи компании

---

## Guidelines

### What to Include

- Clear, concise notes
- Key decisions made
- Action items with owners and due dates
- Important quotes or feedback
- Next steps

### What NOT to Include

- Sensitive client information (use separate secure location)
- Personal opinions (keep notes objective)
- Verbatim transcripts (summarize instead)

### Best Practices

1. **Take notes in real-time** or immediately after meeting
2. **Assign owners** to all action items
3. **Share notes** with participants within 24 hours
4. **Follow up** on action items
5. **Link to related documents** when relevant
